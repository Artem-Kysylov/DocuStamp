How to Sign an Invoice Without Adobe Acrobat

Published: May 2026 • Reading time: 2 min • 100% Private

Your client just replied: "Can you send the signed invoice?" You exported a PDF from your accounting tool, but Adobe Acrobat wants a subscription, DocuSign wants an account, and printing-signing-scanning feels like 2009. If you need to sign an invoice without Adobe Acrobat, you are not looking for enterprise e-signature infrastructure — you need a clean PDF with your signature on it, ready to attach and send.

DocStamp is a browser-native utility built for exactly this moment. No install, no login wall for everyday use, and no file upload to a remote service. Open the app, drop your invoice PDF, and sign it where the "Authorised signature" line lives — or wherever your template expects it. The signature is embedded into the PDF export, so your client receives a normal document their accounts payable team can file without fuss. For freelancers, boutique agencies, and small vendors, that is the entire requirement met in under ten seconds.

Walk through it once and the workflow becomes muscle memory. Generate or download your invoice as a PDF from Xero, QuickBooks, FreshBooks, or a spreadsheet export. Load it into DocStamp and scroll to the signature area at the bottom. Draw your signature with a trackpad or mouse, or upload a PNG of your existing sign-off if you prefer consistency across documents. Adjust size and position so it sits cleanly on the line without covering the total amount or payment terms. Hit export, rename the file if your client expects a specific convention (e.g. INV-1042-signed.pdf), and send it through your usual channel.

Skipping Acrobat is not a compromise on professionalism — it is a rational choice when your volume does not justify a £20/month seat and your privacy requirements rule out cloud signing platforms. A locally processed, visibly signed invoice satisfies most B2B approval flows. You keep control of client billing data, avoid vendor lock-in, and close the loop on payment faster. That is the whole point: invoice signing without Adobe should be frictionless, private, and done before your coffee cools.
